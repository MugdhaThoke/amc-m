# User Guide: AMC Compliance Engineering Platform

Welcome to the AMC Compliance Engineering Platform. This guide is designed for compliance officers, fund managers, and risk analysts.

---

## 🔍 1. Compare Tab
The **Compare Tab** allows you to test compliance and portfolio questions across two search engines simultaneously:
- **Left Panel (Traditional RAG)**: Standard document search matching keywords and text similarity.
- **Right Panel (ContextGraph)**: Intelligent hybrid search that traverses SEBI regulations, entity relationships, and fund holdings.

---

## 💬 2. Chat Tab
The **Chat Tab** supports interactive multi-turn dialogue with conversation context carryover:
- View turn-by-turn answers from both engines.
- Save and resume sessions from the sidebar session history.

---

## 📊 3. Analytics Tab
- **Ontology & Graph Explorer**: Click on nodes (Regulations, Schemes, Entities) to explore relationship chains and see why documents were selected.
- **Run History & Performance**: View average token savings and latency reductions achieved by ContextGraph.
