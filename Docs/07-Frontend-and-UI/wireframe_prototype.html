What are the liquidity requirements for overnight funds as mandated by SEBI?
✓ Verified against SEBI Circular Master Compendium
Under SEBI Mutual Fund Regulations [SEBI/HO/IMD/DF4/CIR/P/2019/102], overnight funds must comply with the following liquidity and investment limits:
  • Invest only in debt and money market instruments with residual maturity of 1 business day [Doc: Clause 2.1].
  • Hold a minimum of 0% illiquid securities and zero structured debt or credit enhancements.
  • Exit load is 0% across all holding periods.
Does this also apply to Liquid Funds, or do they require a minimum 20% liquid asset buffer?
⚡ ContextGraph Synthesis (Streaming)
No, liquid funds have a distinct requirement. Under SEBI Circular [SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2020/229], Liquid Funds are mandated to maintain at least 20% of their net assets in liquid assets (such as cash, government securities, T-bills, and repo on G-Secs).
